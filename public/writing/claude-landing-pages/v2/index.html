Questions

The short answers.

What does Chair cost me?

Nothing to browse and nothing to book. Salons pay to list their diary; the person getting the haircut is never charged a booking fee.

Do I need an account?

Not to look around. To confirm a booking, yes — the salon needs a name and a phone number so they know who is sitting down at 11:15.

Can I cancel?

Yes, in the app, free, up to the cut-off shown on the booking. Each salon sets its own window and you see it before you confirm, not after.

Is my usual stylist on here?

Search their name. If they aren't listed yet, you can send them an invite from inside the app and we'll take it from there.

Which cities are live?

We open one city at a time, salon by salon, so that what you see in the app is a diary someone is actually keeping. Enter your postcode in the app to see what's near you today.
