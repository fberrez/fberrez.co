11:30 The gap

Thirty-five minutes is a blow dry.

The moment a gap opens, whether it is processing time or a cancellation at ten to two, Column texts the three people on the waitlist whose service actually fits it. First to reply takes the slot and leaves a deposit on the way through. If nobody bites in twelve minutes, the gap goes out to everyone.

14:15 The card

The card remembers what your memory shouldn’t have to.

Every formula, every developer volume, every timing, kept against the client and not in a stylist’s head. When Ines is off with a cold, whoever picks up the client can see exactly what went in her hair in January and what she said about it afterwards.

Column also runs the patch test clock. Book a colour service on a client whose test is out of date and the diary stops you, offers the patch test slot instead, and texts her the reminder. Your insurer will want the log; it exports in one click.
