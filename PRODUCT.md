# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People evaluating Florent's work, including peers, collaborators, and potential clients or employers. They should understand what he makes and reach the relevant project or contact link immediately.

## Product Purpose

A compact personal portfolio that identifies Florent, lists his software, and provides direct ways to contact or follow his work.

## Brand Personality

Technical, quiet, and exact. The interface should feel personal and handcrafted without becoming decorative.

## Anti-references

No long introductions, blog content, dashboards, cards, tables, dividers, ornamental sections, or generic portfolio filler. Avoid visual effects that compete with the text.

## Design Principles

- Keep only information that helps a visitor understand the work or follow a link.
- Let the plain `fberrez` wordmark carry the identity.
- Prefer plain text, bullets, and links over interface furniture.
- Make every alignment and spacing choice deliberate.
- Keep project status visible without making it dominant.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic headings, lists, navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, and useful screen-reader labels. Do not depend on color alone to communicate project status.
